These upstream sources are offered for the bundled native components, including
MPL-2.0-covered libdatachannel/libjuice source. StreamVault uses unmodified
upstream Maven binaries. Exact native build settings and dependency revisions
are recorded in the libtorrent4j v2.1.0-38 source tree and its submodules.
